# Code of conduct

Author: 𝓐.𝓒.𝓑

TINVO is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it (in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) is expected to make it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be considerate. Assume the other person means well, and say what you mean plainly.
- Keep criticism on the work: the code, the design, the idea. Say what is wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Accept being told you are wrong, and say so when you were.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and give enough detail to act on.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or attacks on a person or a group, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, 𝓐.𝓒.𝓑 ([@IACBI](https://github.com/IACBI)), decides what crosses the line. Comments, commits and issues that do may be edited, hidden or removed, and the person warned or blocked from the project, temporarily or for good, depending on how serious and how repeated it is.

To report something, mention @IACBI in an issue and ask for a private way to talk, without describing the incident there. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code applies in every space the project runs, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
